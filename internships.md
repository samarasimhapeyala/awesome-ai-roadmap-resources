# Internships and jobs

> Where to apply for AI, machine learning and data internships and jobs: platforms, companies and labs, IIT summer programmes and research fellowships. Links go to official sites.

_Programmes, eligibility, stipends and dates change every year, and some programmes pause. Always confirm on the official page before you plan around one. Several links open the institute's main site, because programme pages move every year: look there for the internship or summer programme notice._

## IIT summer research internships

Most IITs run summer research internships for students from other institutions. Application windows are usually in the first months of the year, so start early.

| Programme | Host | Who it is for | Applications usually |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **[SRIP: Summer Research Internship Programme](https://srip.iitgn.ac.in/)** | IIT Gandhinagar | Undergraduate and postgraduate students | Around March, for a summer internship |
| **[Summer Fellowship Programme](https://www.iitm.ac.in/)** | IIT Madras | Students from colleges other than the IITs | Around February to March, for the summer |
| **[SURE: Summer Undergraduate Research Exposure](https://iith.ac.in/)** | IIT Hyderabad | Undergraduate students from other institutions | Around March, for the summer |
| **[SURGE: Summer Undergraduate Research Graduate Excellence](https://www.iitk.ac.in/)** | IIT Kanpur | Indian students from other institutions (administered by the Centre for Continuing Education) | Early in the year, for the summer |
| **[IITK-RISE: Research Internships in Science and Engineering](https://iitk.ac.in/oir/iitk-rise-internship-programme)** | IIT Kanpur | International students | Check the programme page |
| **[Research Internship Awards](https://www.iitb.ac.in/)** | IIT Bombay | External students from recognised institutes | Has opened around August to September in the past |
| **[Summer research internships](https://home.iitd.ac.in/)** | IIT Delhi | Varies by department | Check the Academics and internship pages |

## Research fellowships and open-source programmes

- **[Science Academies' Summer Research Fellowship (SRFP)](https://www.ias.ac.in/)**: Run jointly by the Indian Academy of Sciences, INSA and NASI. Around two months with a guide at a research institute, including IITs. Applications have closed around late in the year for the following year.
- **[Indian National Science Academy (INSA)](https://www.insaindia.res.in/)**: One of the three academies behind the SRFP.
- **[Google Summer of Code](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/)**: Paid open-source projects with mentors, open to students and newcomers.
- **[MLH Fellowship](https://fellowship.mlh.io/)**: Remote open-source and engineering fellowships.
- **[Outreachy](https://www.outreachy.org/)**: Paid internships in open source and open science for people underrepresented in tech.
- **[Mitacs Globalink Research Internship](https://www.mitacs.ca/en/programs/globalink/globalink-research-internship)**: Research internships at Canadian universities for students from India and other countries.

## How to improve your chances

- **Start early.** Many summer research programmes take applications months ahead. Set reminders for the windows above.
- **Show your work.** Build two or three solid projects (see [projects.md](projects.md)) and keep them on GitHub with a clear README.
- **For research internships,** read a professor's recent papers and send a short, specific email about what you would like to work on and what you have already built.
- **Contribute to open source.** Programmes like Google Summer of Code are a path to real experience and mentors.
- **Keep your resume to one page** and tailor it to each role. Lead with projects and results.
- **Practise interviews** with the roadmap and the interview question PDFs in this repo.

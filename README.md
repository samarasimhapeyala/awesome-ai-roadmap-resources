## 🗺️ The roadmap

## 📚 Free handbooks and books

_30 books their authors publish free online. We only link to the official source._

### Math & statistics

- **[Mathematics for Machine Learning](https://mml-book.github.io/)**: Marc Peter Deisenroth, A. Aldo Faisal, Cheng Soon Ong (Cambridge University Press, 2020) · _Beginner_ · Free PDF from the authors
  <br>Linear algebra, calculus, probability and optimisation, written for people heading into ML.
- **[Think Stats (3rd edition)](https://allendowney.github.io/ThinkStats/)**: Allen B. Downey · _Beginner_ · Creative Commons licence · open licence
  <br>Statistics for programmers, taught with Python and real datasets instead of formulas first.

### Machine learning

- **[An Introduction to Statistical Learning (Python & R editions)](https://www.statlearning.com/)**: Gareth James, Daniela Witten, Trevor Hastie, Robert Tibshirani, Jonathan Taylor (Springer, 2023) · _Beginner_ · Free PDF from the authors
  <br>The friendliest route into regression, classification, trees, SVMs and resampling, with hands-on labs.
- **[The Elements of Statistical Learning](https://hastie.su.domains/ElemStatLearn/)**: Trevor Hastie, Robert Tibshirani, Jerome Friedman (Springer, 2nd edition, 2009) · _Advanced_ · Free PDF from the authors
  <br>The mathematical deep end of ISL, and still the reference for classical machine learning.
- **[Probabilistic Machine Learning: An Introduction](https://probml.github.io/pml-book/book1.html)**: Kevin P. Murphy (MIT Press, 2022) · _Intermediate_ · CC BY-NC-ND licence · open licence
  <br>A modern, thorough tour of machine learning through a probabilistic lens.
- **[Interpretable Machine Learning](https://christophm.github.io/interpretable-ml-book/)**: Christoph Molnar · _Intermediate_ · CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 · open licence
  <br>SHAP, LIME, partial dependence and other ways to explain what a model is doing.
- **[Feature Engineering and Selection](https://www.feat.engineering/)**: Max Kuhn, Kjell Johnson (CRC Press, 2019) · _Intermediate_ · Free to read online
  <br>How to turn raw data into features that models can use, and how to choose between them.
- **[Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction](http://incompleteideas.net/book/the-book-2nd.html)**: Richard S. Sutton, Andrew G. Barto (MIT Press, 2nd edition, 2018) · _Intermediate_ · Free PDF from the authors
  <br>The foundation text for reinforcement learning, and useful background for RLHF.

### Deep learning

- **[Dive into Deep Learning](https://d2l.ai/)**: Aston Zhang, Zachary C. Lipton, Mu Li, Alexander J. Smola (Cambridge University Press, 2023) · _Beginner_ · CC BY-SA 4.0 (text) · open licence
  <br>An interactive deep learning book with runnable PyTorch code for every chapter.
- **[Neural Networks and Deep Learning](http://neuralnetworksanddeeplearning.com/)**: Michael Nielsen · _Beginner_ · CC BY-NC 3.0 · open licence
  <br>Builds real intuition for backpropagation and training, starting from a single neuron.
- **[Deep Learning for Coders with fastai and PyTorch](https://github.com/fastai/fastbook)**: Jeremy Howard, Sylvain Gugger (O'Reilly, 2020) · _Beginner_ · Free notebooks for personal, non-commercial use
  <br>Code-first deep learning, published by the authors as Jupyter notebooks.
- **[Deep Learning](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/)**: Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio, Aaron Courville (MIT Press, 2016) · _Intermediate_ · Free to read online
  <br>The classic theory text on neural networks: optimisation, regularisation, CNNs and sequence models.
- **[Understanding Deep Learning](https://udlbook.github.io/udlbook/)**: Simon J. D. Prince (MIT Press, 2023) · _Intermediate_ · Free PDF from the author
  <br>Modern deep learning from the basics to transformers and diffusion models, with outstanding figures.
- **[Deep Learning: Foundations and Concepts](https://www.bishopbook.com/)**: Christopher M. Bishop, Hugh Bishop (Springer, 2024) · _Intermediate_ · Free to read online
  <br>A rigorous modern textbook from the author of Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning.
- **[The Little Book of Deep Learning](https://fleuret.org/francois/lbdl.html)**: François Fleuret · _Intermediate_ · Creative Commons, non-commercial · open licence
  <br>A short, phone-sized summary of modern deep learning, ideal for revision.

### LLMs & NLP

- **[Speech and Language Processing (3rd edition draft)](https://web.stanford.edu/~jurafsky/slp3/)**: Daniel Jurafsky, James H. Martin · _Intermediate_ · Free draft chapters from the authors
  <br>The standard NLP textbook, updated with transformers and large language models.
- **[Foundations of Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.09223)**: Tong Xiao, Jingbo Zhu (arXiv, 2025) · _Intermediate_ · Free on arXiv
  <br>Pre-training, generative models, prompting and alignment, covered in one book.
- **[The RLHF Book](https://rlhfbook.com/)**: Nathan Lambert · _Advanced_ · Free online
  <br>Reward models, preference tuning and RLHF, explained by a researcher who builds them.
- **[Hugging Face LLM Course](https://huggingface.co/learn/llm-course/chapter1/1)**: Hugging Face · _Beginner_ · Free online course
  <br>Transformers, tokenisers, fine-tuning and sharing models with the Hugging Face libraries.

### Data science & SQL

- **[Python Data Science Handbook](https://jakevdp.github.io/PythonDataScienceHandbook/)**: Jake VanderPlas (O'Reilly, 2016) · _Beginner_ · Text CC BY-NC-ND, code MIT · open licence
  <br>NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib and scikit-learn: the everyday data science toolkit.
- **[Data Science: A First Introduction](https://datasciencebook.ca/)**: Tiffany Timbers, Trevor Campbell, Melissa Lee (CRC Press, 2022) · _Beginner_ · CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 · open licence
  <br>A gentle start to data analysis, visualisation and prediction, using R.
- **[Data Science at the Command Line (2nd edition)](https://jeroenjanssens.com/dsatcl/)**: Jeroen Janssens (O'Reilly, 2021) · _Intermediate_ · CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 · open licence
  <br>Obtain, scrub, explore and model data quickly with command-line tools.
- **[Select Star SQL](https://selectstarsql.com/)**: Zi Chong Kao · _Beginner_ · Free online
  <br>An interactive book that takes you from your first SELECT to joins and window functions.

### MLOps & engineering

- **[Machine Learning Engineering Open Book](https://github.com/stas00/ml-engineering)**: Stas Bekman · _Advanced_ · CC BY-SA 4.0 · open licence
  <br>Training and serving large models: GPUs, networking, storage, debugging and scaling.
- **[Made With ML](https://madewithml.com/)**: Goku Mohandas · _Intermediate_ · Free online
  <br>Design, develop, deploy and iterate on production ML systems, end to end.
- **[Site Reliability Engineering](https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/)**: Betsy Beyer, Chris Jones, Jennifer Petoff, Niall Richard Murphy (eds.) (O'Reilly, 2016) · _Intermediate_ · CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 · open licence
  <br>How Google runs reliable production systems: SLOs, on-call and incidents, the basis of good MLOps.

### Causal inference & forecasting

- **[Forecasting: Principles and Practice (3rd edition)](https://otexts.com/fpp3/)**: Rob J Hyndman, George Athanasopoulos (OTexts, 2021) · _Beginner_ · Free online
  <br>Time-series forecasting done properly, from baselines to ARIMA and evaluation.
- **[Causal Inference: The Mixtape](https://mixtape.scunning.com/)**: Scott Cunningham (Yale University Press, 2021) · _Intermediate_ · Free online
  <br>Diff-in-diff, instrumental variables, regression discontinuity and synthetic control, with code.
- **[Causal Inference for the Brave and True](https://matheusfacure.github.io/python-causality-handbook/landing-page.html)**: Matheus Facure · _Intermediate_ · Free online
  <br>Causal inference in Python with a light touch, from A/B tests to heterogeneous effects.

### Interview prep

- **[Introduction to Machine Learning Interviews](https://huyenchip.com/ml-interviews-book/)**: Chip Huyen · _Beginner_ · Free online
  <br>How ML interviews work from both sides of the table, with practice questions across ML topics.


## 🛠️ Portfolio projects

_32 projects that come up in AI/ML interviews, each with free guides to build it. [See every project with all its resources →](projects.md)_

### Machine learning

- **Titanic survival prediction** · _Beginner_: A full tabular ML workflow: cleaning, feature engineering, cross-validation and a leaderboard submission.
- **House price regression** · _Beginner_: A regression model on 79 features, comparing linear models with regularisation against gradient boosting.
- **Customer churn prediction with explanations** · _Intermediate_: A churn classifier that explains each prediction with SHAP, plus a short write-up of who to target and why.
- **Credit card fraud detection** · _Intermediate_: A model for a dataset with 0.17% positives, comparing resampling, class weights and PR-AUC-based evaluation.
- **Movie recommendation system** · _Intermediate_: Collaborative filtering and a matrix-factorisation model, evaluated with recall@k against a popularity baseline.
- **Store sales forecasting** · _Intermediate_: Daily sales forecasts for thousands of store-product series, beating a seasonal-naive baseline.
- **End-to-end ML project, deployed** · _Intermediate_: Train a model, wrap it in a web service, containerise it and deploy it, following a free structured course.

### Deep learning

- **Build an autograd engine (micrograd)** · _Beginner_: A tiny automatic differentiation engine and neural net library, written from scratch in about 150 lines.
- **Image classifier in PyTorch** · _Beginner_: A CNN trained on CIFAR-10 or MNIST, with training curves, augmentation and a confusion matrix.
- **Transfer learning on your own images** · _Intermediate_: Fine-tune a pretrained ResNet on a small custom dataset and compare feature extraction with full fine-tuning.
- **Object detection fine-tuning** · _Advanced_: Fine-tune a Mask R-CNN detector on a custom dataset and report mAP.
- **Character-level language model (makemore)** · _Intermediate_: A model that generates new names, built up from bigrams to an MLP to a small transformer.

### NLP & transformers

- **Fine-tune a transformer for text classification** · _Beginner_: Fine-tune a pretrained BERT-style model for sentiment or topic classification with the Hugging Face Trainer.
- **Build a BPE tokenizer** · _Intermediate_: The byte-pair encoding algorithm used by GPT models, trained on your own text.
- **Build GPT from scratch** · _Intermediate_: A decoder-only transformer trained on Shakespeare: self-attention, multi-head attention and residual blocks.
- **Build an LLM from scratch, then fine-tune it** · _Advanced_: Pre-train a small GPT-style model, load open weights, then fine-tune it for classification and instruction following.

### LLMs & GenAI

- **Fine-tune an open LLM with LoRA / QLoRA** · _Intermediate_: Fine-tune a 7–8B open model on a narrow task with QLoRA on one GPU, and compare it against prompting the base model.
- **Reproduce GPT-2 (124M)** · _Advanced_: Train GPT-2 small from an empty file: mixed precision, flash attention, gradient accumulation and distributed training.
- **LLM evaluation harness** · _Intermediate_: A test suite for an LLM feature: a graded question set, LLM-as-judge checks and regression runs in CI.
- **Your first GenAI apps** · _Beginner_: Small apps for chat, text generation, image generation and function calling, following a free 21-lesson course.

### RAG

- **Chat with your documents (RAG from scratch)** · _Beginner_: A question-answering app over your own PDFs: chunking, embeddings, a vector store and grounded answers with sources.
- **Advanced RAG with hybrid search and reranking** · _Intermediate_: Add BM25 + vector hybrid search, a cross-encoder reranker and query rewriting, and measure each step's effect.
- **Evaluate a RAG system** · _Intermediate_: Build a test set and measure faithfulness, answer relevance and context recall, then fix the weakest stage.
- **End-to-end RAG application** · _Intermediate_: A complete RAG app with ingestion, search, evaluation and monitoring, built as the capstone of a free course.

### AI agents

- **Tool-using agent from scratch** · _Intermediate_: An agent loop without a framework: the model picks a tool, you run it, feed back the result, and stop safely.
- **Multi-step agent with LangGraph** · _Intermediate_: A stateful agent graph with memory, human-in-the-loop approval and retries for a real task like research or triage.
- **Agents course final project** · _Beginner_: Build and benchmark your own agent across smolagents, LlamaIndex and LangGraph, following a free certified course.
- **Agent design patterns, lesson by lesson** · _Beginner_: Small agents for each core pattern: tool use, planning, multi-agent collaboration, agentic RAG and trust and safety.

### MLOps

- **Production ML pipeline** · _Intermediate_: Design, train, test, serve and iterate on a model with experiment tracking, tests and CI/CD.
- **MLOps capstone: tracking, orchestration, monitoring** · _Intermediate_: A full MLOps project with MLflow tracking, orchestrated retraining, deployment and monitoring.
- **Model monitoring and drift detection** · _Intermediate_: Monitor a deployed model's inputs and predictions, simulate drift, and alert before quality drops.
- **Design a GenAI platform** · _Advanced_: An architecture write-up and prototype of an LLM gateway: routing, caching, guardrails, observability and evals.

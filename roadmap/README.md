# The roadmap

1. [Core Foundations](../roadmap/stage-1-core-foundations.md): The maths, Python and statistics everything else is built on. _(6–8 weeks)_
2. [Data Prep & Architectures](../roadmap/stage-2-data-prep-and-architectures.md): Cleaning, storing and moving data: SQL, pipelines, DSA and reporting. _(6–8 weeks)_
3. [ML & Deployment](../roadmap/stage-3-ml-and-deployment.md): Classical machine learning, the tools around it, and shipping models to production. _(8–10 weeks)_
4. [Advanced AI & Research](../roadmap/stage-4-advanced-ai-and-research.md): Deep learning, research habits, applied NLP, speech and vision, and the foundations of LLMs. _(8–12 weeks)_
5. [Specialized Fields & Translation](../roadmap/stage-5-specialized-fields-and-translation.md): Specialise: RAG, agents, fine-tuning, reinforcement learning, and turning research into products. _(Ongoing)_

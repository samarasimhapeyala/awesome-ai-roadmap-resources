# Contributing

Thanks for helping. This list is only as good as its links.

## What belongs here
- **Free**, high-quality resources: no paywalls, no sign-up walls for the core material.
- Official or author-published sources, not mirrors or pirated copies.
- Up to date and still working.

## How to add or fix something
1. Open an issue with the link, a one-line description, and where it fits (which roadmap stage or section), **or** send a pull request.
2. In a pull request, keep one resource per line, in the existing format, and put it in the right stage or section.
3. Dead link? Say which page it is on, and the replacement if you have one.

## Not accepted
Paid courses, affiliate links, self-promotion without clear value, and anything you don't have the right to share.

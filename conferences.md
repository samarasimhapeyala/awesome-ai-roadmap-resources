# Top AI conferences held every year

> 30 conferences and events, grouped by area, each with its official site.

_These are the conferences held **every year**. Dates and cities change each year, so check the official site. ICCV (odd years) and ECCV (even years) alternate, so they are not listed here; together with CVPR there is a major vision conference every year._

## Read the papers for free

- **[NeurIPS proceedings](https://papers.nips.cc/)**: Every NeurIPS paper, free.
- **[PMLR](https://proceedings.mlr.press/)**: ICML, AISTATS, UAI, COLT and more.
- **[OpenReview](https://openreview.net/)**: ICLR and others, with the public peer reviews.
- **[CVF Open Access](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/)**: CVPR, ICCV and workshop papers.
- **[ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/)**: ACL, EMNLP and the whole NLP literature.
- **[arXiv: cs.LG, cs.CL, cs.CV](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.LG/recent)**: Most papers appear here first.

## Tips

- Submission deadlines are usually several months before the conference, so plan ahead if you want to submit.
- Most papers are free on arXiv or the proceedings sites above, so you can follow a conference without attending.
- Many conferences have workshops, student volunteer programmes and travel grants.

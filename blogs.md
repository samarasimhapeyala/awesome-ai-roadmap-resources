## Researcher and engineer blogs

- **[Lil'Log (Lilian Weng)](https://lilianweng.github.io/)**: Deep, well-referenced posts on LLMs, agents, RL and more.
- **[Andrej Karpathy](https://karpathy.github.io/)**: Classic explainers, including the unreasonable effectiveness of RNNs.
- **[Jay Alammar](https://jalammar.github.io/)**: The Illustrated Transformer and other visual explainers.
- **[Chris Olah (colah's blog)](https://colah.github.io/)**: Understanding LSTMs and neural network intuition.
- **[Chip Huyen](https://huyenchip.com/blog/)**: ML systems, MLOps and building LLM applications.
- **[Sebastian Raschka: Ahead of AI](https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/)**: LLM research explained, with code.
- **[Sebastian Ruder](https://www.ruder.io/)**: NLP research and transfer learning.
- **[Eugene Yan](https://eugeneyan.com/writing/)**: Applied ML, recommender systems and LLM patterns.
- **[Hamel Husain](https://hamel.dev/blog/)**: Practical LLM evals and fine-tuning.
- **[Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/)**: Daily notes on LLMs, tools and prompt injection.

## Must-read posts

- **[The Illustrated Transformer](https://jalammar.github.io/illustrated-transformer/)**: The clearest visual walk-through of the transformer.
- **[The Annotated Transformer](http://nlp.seas.harvard.edu/annotated-transformer/)**: The transformer paper, line by line in code.
- **[The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Recurrent Neural Networks](http://karpathy.github.io/2015/05/21/rnn-effectiveness/)**: A classic on what RNNs can do.
- **[LLM Powered Autonomous Agents](https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2023-06-23-agent/)**: A thorough overview of agent design.
- **[What Is ChatGPT Doing... and Why Does It Work?](https://writings.stephenwolfram.com/2023/02/what-is-chatgpt-doing-and-why-does-it-work/)**: A long, plain-language explanation.
- **[Building LLM applications for production](https://huyenchip.com/2023/04/11/llm-engineering.html)**: The challenges of shipping LLM apps.
- **[Patterns for Building LLM-based Systems & Products](https://eugeneyan.com/writing/llm-patterns/)**: Evals, RAG, fine-tuning, caching and guardrails.
